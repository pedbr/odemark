02 · Why

No database of spots. No crowd.

Every outdoor app on the market is a database of submitted places. The model has a permanent flaw: people who find good ground don't publish it. A shared spot stops being the spot you shared.

Ödemark collects nothing from any crowd. Candidate ground is computed from official Swedish geodata. Every search gets its own answer — two thousand people are never sent to the same lakeshore.

The computation is only possible in countries with freedom to roam. That is why this app hasn't existed until now.

03 · How it computes

Deterministic analysis of government data.

Same input, same answer. The app weighs terrain factors against each other and draws zones where the conditions hold.

Lantmäteriet

National elevation model, 2 m resolution. Height and slope — the basis for everything.

Naturvårdsverket · NMD

National land-cover data. Forest type, wetland, open ground.

Protected areas

National parks, reserves and local rules that restrict camping.

Buildings and roads

Distance to dwellings and drivable roads. The home-privacy zone is respected.

SMHI

Weather as a filter, not a judgement.

06 · Privacy

Nobody can see your places. Including us.

sharing

Does not exist. There is no feature to leave on by mistake.

feed

Does not exist. No followers, no likes, no community to join.

your places

Stored encrypted in your library. Not visible to other users, not to us.

this website

No cookies, no third-party code, no tracking. That is why there is no cookie banner. The network tab confirms it.

To share your spot is to lose it. The app is built so that you can't.

07 · Caveats

The map is a computation. The ground is real.

Zones are suggestions derived from data and your filters — not promises. The app points out where it looks worth walking in. You decide on the ground.

resolution

The elevation model is 2 m coarse. Boulders, ditches and windthrow don't show.

currency

Government data updates in cycles. A fresh clear-cut can be ahead of the map.

weather

A forecast is a forecast. The weather layer filters your search — it doesn't guarantee the night.

on the ground

Signs, local rules and judgement outrank every computed zone. Allemansrätten applies to you, not the app.

The final call is made with your boots on.

08 · Leave no trace

Nobody should see you were there.

The app sends you to ground without human traces. That only works if every visit leaves it unchanged. Seven principles, built on Leave No Trace and adapted to Swedish terrain.

  2. 02 · camp on durable groundrock slab and needle-mat — never lichen
  3. 03 · carry everything outthe organic too
  4. 04 · leave what you findno cairns, no structures
  5. 05 · fire with carestove first · never on rock slab
  6. 06 · respect the wildlifedistance · dogs leashed in nesting season
  7. 07 · be considerateof camps, landowners — and the place
