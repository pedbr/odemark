Allemansrätten

What actually applies when you camp.

Allemansrätten — Sweden's right of public access — is not a statute with paragraphs about tents. It is customary law, written into the constitution, bounded by other legislation. These are the main rules in plain language. Ödemark computes with them; you are responsible for them. When in doubt, Naturvårdsverket's guidance and municipal rules apply, not this page.

The principle

Do not disturb, do not destroy. You may move freely through nature and stay overnight for a day or so, including on land someone else owns. The right rests on leaving the ground as you found it.

How long

A single day or so in the same place — in practice one night or two. Staying longer requires the landowner's permission. Move once too often rather than once too rarely.

Where

  • Not within sight of a dwelling. The home-privacy zone (hemfridszonen) has no fixed metre limit; Ödemark computes conservatively with at least 150 metres, and further where the terrain is open.
  • Not on plots, farmyards or cultivated land — fields, plantations, replanted forest.
  • Not where municipal ordinances prohibit camping, for instance in some recreation areas and at bathing sites.

Protected areas

National parks and nature reserves have their own rules. Many reserves prohibit camping entirely or restrict it to designated sites. Ödemark reads Naturvårdsverket's register of protected nature and excludes areas whose rules prevent camping — but signage on the ground always takes precedence.

Fire

Open fires are allowed with normal care on ground where fire cannot spread — gravel, sand, bare rock without moss. Never directly on a rock slab: it cracks. During a fire ban (eldningsförbud) the ban applies regardless of how careful you are; camping stoves are usually exempt, check the municipal decision. The app shows current fire bans as a separate data layer.

Water and waste

You may take water from lakes and streams, but not disturb private wells or water supplies. Everything you carry in, you carry out. Bury what should be buried, well away from water.

Wildlife and vegetation

Do not cut living branches for bedding or fires. Keep your distance from nesting birds in spring and early summer. Dogs must be under strict control 1 March – 20 August — in practice, leashed.

How Ödemark computes with this

The zones you see in the app are pre-computed against the elevation model, land cover, protected areas, and distance to buildings and roads. What cannot be computed — signs, temporary rules, judgement on the ground — is yours. That is why the app stops at a zone and never at a point.

Sources

Naturvårdsverket's guidance on allemansrätten · the Swedish Environmental Code ch. 7 · local rules from each municipality and county administrative board. This page is checked against Naturvårdsverket's guidance and is not legal advice.
