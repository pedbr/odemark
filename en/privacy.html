Privacy

Short, because it is simple.

This website

Sets no cookies. Loads no code, fonts or images from third parties. No ad tech, no pixels, no fingerprinting. That is why there is no cookie banner — there is nothing to consent to. Analytics, if enabled, run cookie-free and self-hosted (Plausible) and cannot identify individual visitors.

The app

Your saved places — coordinates, notes, photos — are stored encrypted in your library. They are not shown to other users. They are not shown to us. There is no sharing feature, no feed, and no content moderation, because no content is visible to anyone else.

Searches are computed against pre-computed zone data. We do not store where you search, and zone results are not linked to your account.

The region email list

If you leave your email to hear when your region is computed, one row is stored: address and region. It is used for a single message and then deleted. No resale, no marketing.

Your rights

GDPR applies. You can request access, correction and deletion via hej@odemark.app. Deleting your account deletes the library permanently — we cannot restore it, because we cannot read it.
