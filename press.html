One line

Ödemark computes unfound camping ground from public geodata, so you can plan a trip somewhere nobody goes — then keeps the places you find private, permanently.

Facts

  • Planning tool for wild camping under allemansrätten, Sweden's right of public access.
  • No user-submitted spots. Candidate ground is computed from official Swedish geodata: Lantmäteriet elevation, national land-cover data, protected areas, buildings and roads.
  • Results are zones, never pins. Saved places are private, permanently — no sharing feature exists.
  • Deterministic analysis. Same input, same answer.
  • Coverage begins with Stockholms län and rolls out county by county.
  • iOS and Android. Founded in Sweden by Pedro Brás.

Name and spelling

Ödemark ("wilderness, waste land"). The Ö keeps its diacritic in all display text. Plain-text fallback where diacritics are unavailable: Odemark. Domains: odemark.se (canonical), ödemark.se.
